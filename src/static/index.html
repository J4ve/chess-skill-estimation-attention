Live status

Suspicion score

Suspicion score is a supplementary flag for human review, not proof of cheating. It is S_att, the attention-weighted average rating-point gap between the model's per-move estimate and the player's baseline over the whole game. In thesis evaluation, this score separated engine-substituted games only weakly (ROC-AUC 0.555 on synthetic data).
